WHY YOUR SUPPORT MATTERS

When you donate, you directly fund travel, equipment, lodging, and registration costs for competitive collegiate climbing. Donors like you keep our athletes on the wall and competing nationally. Join the Rutgers Rock Climbing Family today and support our passion!

WHERE YOUR DONATION GOES

01

Competition Fees

Covers USA Climbing registration fees and entry into collegiate regional & national comps.

02

Travel & Lodging

Funds van rentals, gas, and lodging for out-of-state travel to national competitions.

03

Gear & Safety

Provides team ropes, crash pads, chalk, and essential safety equipment for training.

04

Athlete Support

Reduces out-of-pocket costs so every climber can represent Rutgers.

READY TO MAKE AN IMPACT?

All donations are processed securely through the official Rutgers University Foundation page.
